Introduction

Welcome to FaceCard ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application FaceCard – AI Dermatologist (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Face Data Privacy

FaceCard's core feature is AI skin analysis of photos of your face. This section describes our collection, use, disclosure, retention, and deletion of face data, and how to revoke your consent.

What face data we collect and why

  • Front-facing and side-profile facial photographs that you capture in the App during a skin scan.
  • Skin condition assessments generated by AI from those photographs (numerical scores and text for hydration, texture, tone, puffiness, and similar visible characteristics).

All intended uses of face data: (1) AI analysis of visible skin characteristics; (2) tracking your skin's progress between scans; (3) generating personalized skincare routines and product recommendations. For example, when you complete a scan, your photos are analyzed to produce your skin scores and that day's recommendations.

We do not use face data for facial recognition, identification, or authentication of any person, and we do not create biometric templates or identifiers.

Face data sharing and disclosure

  • OpenAI: your facial photographs are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to OpenAI's API solely to perform the skin analysis. OpenAI processes the images to generate the analysis and, under its API terms, does not use API-submitted images to train its models.
  • No other third parties: we do not share, sell, or disclose face data to any other company, advertiser, or data broker.

Third-party protection: any third party with whom we share face data is required to provide the same or equal protection of your face data as described in this Privacy Policy.

Face data retention

  • Facial photographs: processed transiently for analysis and not retained on our servers. Photographs exist on your device during capture and are transmitted for analysis; they are not stored server-side after the analysis completes.
  • Analysis results (scores and recommendations, not images): retained while your account exists so the App can show your progress over time.

Face data deletion and revoking consent

  • Delete in the App: Settings → Delete Account permanently deletes your account and all associated data, including all skin analysis results, immediately.
  • Delete by email: you may also request deletion by emailing support@vesperscale.com; we complete requests within 30 days.
  • Revoking consent: you can revoke consent for face data collection at any time by simply not using the scan feature (no face data is ever collected outside a scan you start), and you can revoke consent for our continued storage of past analysis results by deleting your account as described above.

Information We Collect

Personal information you provide

Account information: email address, name, phone number, age, gender, ethnicity (optional), password (encrypted; never stored in plain text), Apple ID (if using Sign in with Apple).

Profile and preferences: skincare focus areas and goals, lifestyle information, current skincare routine level, environment/climate information.

Facial images: as described in the Face Data Privacy section above.

Subscription information: subscription status and history. Payment details are processed by Apple and RevenueCat; we never see or store card numbers.

Automatically collected information

Device information: device type and model, operating system version, app version, push notification token (if you enable notifications).

Usage data: features used, scan history and dates, error logs and crash reports.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide our services: analyze your skin with AI, generate personalized recommendations, track progress over time, deliver routines and product suggestions.
  • To improve the App: understand feature usage, fix technical issues, improve analysis accuracy.
  • To communicate with you: scan and routine reminder notifications, customer support, important account or subscription updates.
  • For subscription management: process purchases through Apple's In-App Purchase system and verify entitlements.

Business transfers: if FaceCard is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred; we will provide notice before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

Data Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+), encryption at rest, industry-standard authentication, row-level access controls on our database, and least-privilege access. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use commercially reasonable safeguards.
